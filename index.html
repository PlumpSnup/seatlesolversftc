About the team

Who we are

We are Deadly, a team of middle and high school students from across Dubai who share one obsession: robots.

Competing is only half of it. We want to make STEM something every student in our community can try, not just the ones who already love it.

Our mission: learn real engineering skills, bring STEM to our community through hands-on programs, and have a great time doing it.

What we do

More than a robot

Compete

We design, build and code a new robot every season for the FIRST Tech Challenge.

Teach

Workshops and classes that introduce younger students to robotics, CAD and programming.

Share

We publish our code and guides so other teams can learn from what we build.
