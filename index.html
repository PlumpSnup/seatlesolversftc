About the team

Who we are

We are team #34237 Deadly, a legacy robotics team in Dubai, founded at Unique World Robotics.

We are self-funded and a community team, with members from many different schools. In our first season, DECODE 2025–26, we were on the winning alliance at the Dubai Scrimmage, the UAE Regional Scrimmage and the UAE National Championship, and qualified for the Istanbul Premier Event.

Our mission: make robotics accessible in our community and around the world, and bring more students into FIRST programs.

What we do

More than a robot

Compete

We design, build and code a new robot every season for the FIRST Tech Challenge.

Teach

Workshops, scrimmages and mentoring for new teams, including our sister team, Deadlier.

Inspire

We volunteer as FIRST Ambassadors and work to bring robotics to groups that are often overlooked.
