Funding

From 3 to 6 sponsors

In DECODE we had only three sponsors, and our limited budget held back our outreach. This season we doubled that to six, and we're sending 50+ cold emails for discounted services and funding.

To be less dependent on yearly sponsorships, we partnered with FRC Tees to launch our own online store with Deadly merch. Alya and Asiro run it.

Lessons from DECODE

  • Cost limits outreach

    Funding shortfalls cut our outreach opportunities and reduced our exposure.

  • Keeping sponsors isn't enough

    Sponsor retention matters, but on its own it can't cover what we need.

  • Budget follows goals

    We now align the budget with our outreach and robot goals separately.
