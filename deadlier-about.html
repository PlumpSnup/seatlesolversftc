About Deadlier

Who we are

Deadlier is the junior sister team of Deadly #34237, based in Dubai, UAE.

Our story

Founded by Deadly

Deadly founded Deadlier in the 2026–27 season as part of its 3-year sustainability plan. We launched with 7 students.

Deadly is losing experienced members as seniors graduate, so it needed a way to train the next generation. Deadlier is that way: new members learn how FTC works here before they move up to Deadly.

Our mission

To give students who are new to FTC a place to learn, build and compete, with help from the members who came before them.
