Who we are

Deadly is FIRST Tech Challenge Team #34237, a self-funded community robotics team based in Dubai, UAE.

Our story

Built in Dubai

We are team #34237 Deadly, a legacy robotics team in Dubai, founded at Unique World Robotics. We are self-funded and a community team, with members from many different schools.

Our first season was DECODE 2025–26. We ran our first season, hosted a scrimmage, secured three sponsors and launched this website. This season we founded our sister team, Deadlier.

2026–27 season

Our goals

Improving recruitment & onboarding

We ran a dual-track recruitment process, bringing in experienced members from this year's FGC teams and newcomers from our preseason workshop, to grow a well-rounded team ready for the season.

Expanding skill sets

With veterans and newcomers on one team, everyone set out to learn something new. Veterans branched into new areas and taught the newcomers, who built their fundamentals.

Building the coolest robot

Mentor collaboration, structured design reviews and data-driven testing, using engineering design principles that make the robot reliable as well as cool.

Inspiring the next generation

Engage younger students by sharing our passion and demonstrating FIRST values: host 2–3 local scrimmages and FGC Nationals, and open a dedicated robotics lab for the RAIL Project (Deadly FLL India).

How we work

Roles & leadership

At the start of the season we built a skills matrix of each member's abilities, interests and season goals, then picked roles that match our strengths and the areas we want to grow in. It also showed the skills we still needed: socials, finite element analysis, quantitatively motivated design and vision systems coding.

Members take ownership of subsystems, lead design reviews and mentor one another, and everyone leads their own outreach initiative.

We plan the season in a Gantt chart that we update every week. Overdue tasks are reassigned or re-scoped at the weekly review.

Mentoring

Learning from each other

Older members and alumni teach skills to younger members on both Deadly and Deadlier. In the offseason, Pranav taught CAD, Abbas taught coding, and Aiz, Asiro and Jihad helped with outreach. Leftover budget from last season paid for skill development projects.
