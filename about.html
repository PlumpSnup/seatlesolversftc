About us

Who we are

Deadly is FIRST Tech Challenge Team #34237, a student-led robotics team based in Dubai, UAE.

Our story

Built in Dubai

Tell your story here: how the team started, who founded it and what your first season was like.

Today our members come from middle and high schools across Dubai. We meet every week to design, build, program and test, and we spend just as much time sharing what we learn.

Our mission

To learn real engineering skills, bring STEM to our community through hands-on programs, and have a great time doing it.
